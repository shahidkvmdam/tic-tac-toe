Delete Your Tic Tac Toe Account and Data

Developer: shahidkvmdam

App: Tic Tac Toe

You can request deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to shahid.kvmdam@gmail.com from the account you used to sign in.
  2. Subject: Tic Tac Toe account deletion request
  3. Include your display name or registered phone number/email used in the app.
  4. We will process your request within 30 days and confirm by email.

What data is deleted

  • Account profile (display name, avatar, phone number, email)
  • Authentication credentials and Firebase Authentication account
  • Chat messages and sent/received images
  • Game history and leaderboard entries tied to your account
  • Friend requests and invitations
  • FCM push notification tokens

What data may be kept

  • Aggregated, non-identifiable gameplay statistics that cannot be linked to you.
  • Data we are required to keep by law for a limited time.

Additional retention period

Your data will be deleted within 30 days of receiving your request. Backups may take up to 90 days to be fully removed from our systems.
