Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Tic Tac Toe mobile application collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the app.

Information We Collect

When you use the app, we may collect the following information:

  • Email address — used to create and identify your account (via Google Sign-In or Firebase Authentication).
  • Display name and profile picture / avatar — shown to other players during online matches, tournaments, chat, and friend requests.
  • Device information — including device type and operating system version to improve app performance.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token — used to send push notifications for new chat messages, friend requests, and game invitations, even when the app is closed.

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide online multiplayer game services.
  • Enable chat, friend requests, tournament matchmaking, and game invitations.
  • Send push notifications for new messages and game requests.
  • Protect against abuse, fraud, and cheating.
  • Maintain and improve app functionality.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Firebase services provided by Google. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. All authentication and database access is protected by Firebase security rules.

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In / Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Cloud Firestore
  • Firebase Cloud Storage
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging

Children's Privacy

The app is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us to remove it.

Your Choices

  • You can delete your account or data by contacting us.
  • You can disable push notifications through your device settings at any time.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
